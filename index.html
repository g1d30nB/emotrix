The layer between signal and meaning

Emotion detection tells you someone's face moved. Emotrix tells you what it means, why it matters, and what to do about it.

The problem

Emotion AI is either locked down or left to you

The market has consolidated into closed ecosystems that lock you to a single vendor, or raw APIs that hand you detection scores and expect you to build the intelligence yourself. There is no open, production-ready layer that turns emotion signals into something your team can actually use. That's what we're building.

What Emotrix does

Engine-agnostic

Swap signal providers without touching your integration. Your intelligence layer isn't locked to any single vendor's roadmap, pricing, or limitations. Start with one engine, add others, switch entirely. Your data and insights stay.

Contextual intelligence

The same micro-expression means different things in different contexts. A flash of surprise during a usability test is not the same as surprise in a vehicle cockpit. Emotrix interprets through domain-specific lenses, so you get meaning, not numbers.

Production-ready

Session management, multi-participant analysis, emotional journey visualisation, comparative analytics, and compliance tooling. The operational infrastructure that turns a research API into enterprise software.

Domain-configurable

One platform, many applications. Configure the intelligence layer for your industry and use case without rebuilding the stack. The interpretation changes; the infrastructure doesn't.

Multimodal by design

Voice

Prosody, pitch, rhythm, and micro-hesitations. What the voice reveals that words alone never will.

+

Face

Micro-expressions, action units, and emotional trajectories across 48+ dimensions of facial affect.

Cross-modal fusion and confidence scoring. When face and voice agree, certainty is high. When they diverge, something important is happening.
